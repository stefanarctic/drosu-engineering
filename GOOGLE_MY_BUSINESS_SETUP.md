# Google My Business Setup Guide for DROSU Engineering

## 🎯 **What is Google My Business?**
Google My Business (GMB) is a free tool that helps you manage how your business appears in Google Search and Maps. It's crucial for local SEO success.

## 📋 **Step-by-Step Setup**

### **Step 1: Access Google My Business**
1. Go to [https://business.google.com](https://business.google.com)
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Click "Manage now"

#### **Verification Methods**
