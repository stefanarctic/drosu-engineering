# Google Search Console Setup Guide for DROSU Engineering

## 🎯 **What is Google Search Console?**
Google Search Console is a free tool that helps you monitor and maintain your website's presence in Google Search results. It's essential for SEO success.

## 📋 **Step-by-Step Setup**

### **Step 1: Access Google Search Console**
1. Go to [https://search.google.com/search-console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Click "Start now"

### **Step 2: Add Your Property**
1. **Property Type**: Choose "Domain" (recommended) or "URL prefix"
2. **Domain**: Enter `drosuengineering.com` (without www)
3. Click "Continue"

### **Step 3: Verify Ownership**
Choose one of these verification methods:

#### **Option A: HTML Tag (Recommended)**
1. Copy the HTML tag provided by Google
2. Add it to your website's `<head>` section
3. I'll help you add this to your site

#### **Option B: DNS Record**
1. Add a TXT record to your domain's DNS
2. Wait for DNS propagation (up to 48 hours)

#### **Option C: Google Analytics**
1. If you have Google Analytics, use that account

### **Step 4: Submit Your Sitemap**
1. Once verified, go to "Sitemaps" in the left menu
2. Enter: `https://www.drosuengineering.com/sitemap.xml`
3. Click "Submit"

### **Step 5: Request Indexing**
1. Go to "URL Inspection" in the left menu
2. Enter your main URLs:
   - `https://www.drosuengineering.com/`
   - `https://www.drosuengineering.com/#services`
   - `https://www.drosuengineering.com/#about`
   - `https://www.drosuengineering.com/#contact`
3. Click "Request Indexing" for each

## 📊 **What to Monitor in Search Console**

### **Performance Tab**
- **Queries**: What people search for to find your site
- **Pages**: Which pages get the most impressions
- **Countries**: Where your traffic comes from
- **Devices**: Mobile vs desktop performance

### **Index Coverage**
- **Submitted URLs**: How many pages you've submitted
- **Indexed URLs**: How many Google has indexed
- **Errors**: Any issues preventing indexing

### **Enhancements**
- **Core Web Vitals**: Page speed metrics
- **Mobile Usability**: Mobile-friendly issues
- **Rich Results**: Structured data performance

## 🔍 **Common Issues & Solutions**

### **"URL not on Google"**
- Submit URL for indexing
- Check robots.txt isn't blocking
- Ensure page is accessible

### **"Mobile usability issues"**
- Test mobile responsiveness
- Fix touch target sizes
- Optimize mobile loading

### **"Core Web Vitals issues"**
- Optimize images
- Minimize CSS/JavaScript
- Enable browser caching
